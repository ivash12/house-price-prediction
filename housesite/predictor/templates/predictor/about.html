About

This is a learning project. It estimates property prices with neural networks built in TensorFlow. Pick a city, enter a few details about a home, and choose its address. The model returns an estimated price based on similar properties in its training data.

What the estimates are based on

  • Amsterdam: about 900 listings from 2021 (asking prices), adjusted upward to roughly match today's market.
  • New York City: a few thousand listings from a public dataset.
  • Paris: about 30,000 apartment sales from 2025, taken from official French government records.

Please read before using the numbers

These estimates are rough and should not be used to buy, sell or value a real property. The datasets are limited. The models only know a property's size, rooms and location, not its condition, floor, view, renovation or the current market. A typical estimate can be off by 10–20% or more, depending on the city. For a real valuation, ask a licensed agent or appraiser.
